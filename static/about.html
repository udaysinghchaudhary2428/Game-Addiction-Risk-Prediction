📊 About Our Model 📊

Understanding the Technology Behind Gaming Addiction Risk Assessment

🤖 Machine Learning Model

Our gaming addiction risk assessment is powered by a Random Forest Classifier, a state-of-the-art ensemble machine learning algorithm. The Random Forest model combines multiple decision trees to provide robust and accurate predictions. This approach significantly reduces overfitting and improves generalization, making it ideal for real-world medical and psychological assessments.

Model Version: 1.0.0

🌳 How Random Forest Works

A Random Forest algorithm works by creating multiple decision trees during training and outputting the class that is the mode of the classes output by individual trees. The key advantages include:

  • High Accuracy: Combines predictions from multiple trees for better accuracy
  • Robustness: Handles missing values and maintains accuracy with large data
  • Interpretability: Provides feature importance scores to understand key factors
  • Generalization: Reduces overfitting through ensemble voting mechanism
  • Non-parametric: Makes no assumptions about data distribution

📈 Input Features

Our model analyzes 9 key features to assess gaming addiction risk:

  • Behavioral: Loss of other interests, withdrawal symptoms, continued despite problems
  • Performance: Academic/work performance level
  • Sleep: Sleep disruption frequency and hours of sleep
  • Social: Social isolation score (1-10)
  • Usage: Daily gaming hours and monthly spending

These features are selected based on clinical research and DSM-5 criteria for Internet Gaming Disorder.

🎯 Risk Classification

The model classifies gaming addiction risk into distinct categories:

🔴 HIGH RISK (Severe)

Strong indicators of gaming addiction requiring immediate professional intervention.

🟠 MEDIUM RISK (Moderate)

Notable gaming addiction indicators requiring monitoring and intervention.

🟢 LOW RISK (Minimal)

Few indicators of gaming addiction with healthy gaming habits maintained.

📚 Model Training & Validation

The Random Forest model was trained using a comprehensive dataset of gaming behavior patterns, clinical assessments, and validated against established addiction criteria. The model employs:

  • Cross-Validation: K-fold cross-validation ensures reliable performance estimates
  • Feature Scaling: Normalization of features for consistent tree splits
  • Hyperparameter Tuning: Optimized parameters for maximum accuracy
  • Train-Test Split: Separate evaluation on unseen data

🔍 Feature Importance

Random Forest provides insight into which features are most influential in predicting gaming addiction:

  • Daily Gaming Hours: Strong indicator of addiction severity
  • Continued Despite Problems: Key clinical criterion for addiction
  • Sleep Disruption: Physical health impact of gaming
  • Academic Performance: Functional impairment measure
  • Social Isolation Score: Relationship and social impact
